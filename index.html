About

I am a research scholar in solar physics at the National Institute of Technology Calicut, India. My research focuses on quasi-periodic pulsations (QPPs) associated with solar flares and eruptions, including their temporal behaviour and signatures across different wavelength ranges.

My work brings together observations from Aditya-L1/HEL1OS and other solar observatories with time-series analysis methods to study oscillatory signals in the solar atmosphere.

Research themes and projects

Hard X-ray QPPs with Aditya-L1/HEL1OS

Statistical investigation of quasi-periodic pulsations in X-class solar flares, including energy-dependent temporal and modulation properties.

Multi-wavelength QPP diagnostics

Comparing oscillatory signatures in hard X-rays, EUV imaging, and ultraviolet observations to investigate flare-associated dynamics across atmospheric diagnostics.

QPPs in CMEs and eruptive solar events

Studying oscillations associated with coronal mass ejections, shock waves, and prominence eruptions using solar observations.

Signal processing and computational analysis

Applying empirical mode decomposition, wavelet methods, Fourier analysis, and related time-series techniques to solar observations and simulated light curves.
