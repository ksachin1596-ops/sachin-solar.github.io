About Me

I am a solar physics researcher interested in the temporal behaviour of solar flares and quasi-periodic pulsations (QPPs). My work combines space-based observations, signal processing, and computational approaches to investigate oscillatory signatures in the solar atmosphere.

This website presents my research interests, publications, projects, and academic activities.
